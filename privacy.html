Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how Content Manager handles information when you use the desktop application and its supported integrations.

2. Information processed

Depending on the features you use, Content Manager may process local video files and publishing information that you choose to provide. When you connect a supported account, the application may receive account identifiers, authorization tokens, and content or publishing information made available by that platform's API.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide the functions you request, such as connecting an authorized account, publishing your selected content, checking publishing status, and retrieving information about your own published content.

4. Local content

Video files selected from your computer are processed for the publishing or management operation you initiate. Content Manager does not require you to upload unrelated files to this website.

5. Third-party platforms

When you connect a third-party platform, information is exchanged with that platform through its APIs according to the permissions you authorize. The platform's own privacy policy also applies to information it processes.

6. Sharing

Content Manager does not sell your personal information. Information is not intentionally disclosed to unrelated third parties except where necessary to provide a feature you request through an authorized third-party integration, to comply with law, or to protect the service and its users.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the information and feature involved. You can disconnect third-party accounts using the available authorization controls, and you can remove local application data using the application's available settings or by removing the application data from your device.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application or its integrations change. The date above identifies the current version published on this site.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method provided with the application or its distribution page.
